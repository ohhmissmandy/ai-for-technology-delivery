# What Are We Missing?

Use this when a project, implementation, decision, or plan feels mostly complete, but you want a second set of eyes before moving forward.

## Prompt

Review the information I provide and identify anything important that may be missing, unclear, assumed, or not fully thought through.

Start with what we know and what we're assuming. Then call out anything that's missing, unclear, dependent on something else, or still waiting on a decision. If something is unclear, don't guess. Identify what needs to be confirmed and why it matters.

Look for gaps across the areas that are relevant to this work, including business goals, scope, requirements, ownership, stakeholders, dependencies, technical needs, integrations, data, security, vendors, testing, UAT, training, operational readiness, support, adoption, resources, timing, and approvals.

Don't force a category just because it's on the list.

For each gap, explain what appears to be missing, why it matters, what could happen if it stays unresolved, and the question or action that would resolve it.

Also look for contradictions. If two requirements, dates, assumptions, stakeholder expectations, or parts of the plan appear to conflict, call that out directly.

Prioritize the findings into:

**Resolve Before Moving Forward**

**Resolve Soon**

**Keep an Eye On**

Finish with the five most important questions I should ask next, ranked in order.

Do not manufacture concerns for the sake of creating a longer list.

## What to provide

Paste the plan, project information, decision, notes, or other material you want reviewed.
