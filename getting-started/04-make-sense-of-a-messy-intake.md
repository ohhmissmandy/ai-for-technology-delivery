# Make Sense of a Messy Intake

Use this when the initial request is scattered across notes, emails, meetings, tickets, or stakeholder conversations.

## Prompt

Review the information below and turn it into a clean initiative brief.

Capture:

* what appears to be requested
* the business problem behind the request
* the desired outcome
* affected users or teams
* known requirements
* constraints
* dependencies
* known risks or concerns
* decisions already made
* decisions still needed
* likely stakeholders
* questions we need answered before planning can move forward

Separate confirmed information from assumptions.

If the information conflicts, flag it instead of choosing the version you think is right.

Finish with a short recommended next step.

Do not invent requirements or fill gaps with what you think the requester probably meant.

## What to provide

Paste the intake material, notes, emails, requests, tickets, or other available information.
