# Find the Holes in These Requirements

Use this when requirements already exist and you want to know whether they are complete enough to build, configure, test, or approve.

## Prompt

Review the requirements below and identify anything that is:

* unclear
* ambiguous
* incomplete
* contradictory
* difficult to test
* missing an acceptance condition
* dependent on another requirement or decision
* based on an unstated assumption
* likely to create different interpretations between business and technical teams

Look for missing requirements around normal workflows, exceptions, error handling, permissions, roles, integrations, data, reporting, notifications, auditability, security, compliance, support, and operational needs where relevant.

Do not create requirements simply because a category exists. Only flag something when the information suggests it matters to this solution.

For each finding, show:

**Requirement or area**

**What is unclear or missing**

**Why it matters**

**Question to resolve it**

**Suggested clarification**, if the existing information supports one

Finish with the requirements or questions that should be resolved before build or configuration begins.

## What to provide

Paste the current business, functional, technical, operational, or other requirements plus any relevant context about the solution.
