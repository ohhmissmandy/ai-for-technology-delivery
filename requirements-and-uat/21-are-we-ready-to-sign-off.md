# Are We Ready to Sign Off?

Use this when UAT is nearing completion and you need to determine whether the evidence actually supports sign-off.

## Prompt

Review the UAT results and assess whether the solution is ready for business sign-off.

Evaluate:

* required scenarios completed
* pass and fail results
* open defects
* defect severity and business impact
* retesting still needed
* requirements without adequate test coverage
* unresolved business questions
* accepted workarounds
* known limitations
* operational impacts
* risks being accepted at sign-off

Separate:

**Complete**

**Complete with accepted limitations or risk**

**Still open**

**Blocking sign-off**

For every open item, explain whether it actually prevents sign-off and why.

Do not assume UAT must be perfect before sign-off. Focus on whether the remaining risk is understood, acceptable, and owned by the appropriate decision-maker.

Also do not recommend sign-off simply because the planned testing window has ended.

Finish with:

**Sign-off recommendation**

**Conditions, if any**

**Risks being accepted**

**Who needs to make the final decision**

## What to provide

Paste the UAT results, test coverage, defect list, retest status, known limitations, workarounds, acceptance criteria, and any current sign-off requirements.
