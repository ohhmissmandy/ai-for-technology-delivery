# Turn Business Needs Into Something We Can Build

Use this when the business knows what it needs to accomplish, but the request has not yet been turned into clear requirements.

## Prompt

Review the business need below and turn it into a structured set of requirements that business and technical teams can review together.

Start by identifying:

* the business problem
* desired outcome
* affected users
* current process, if known
* future process or capability being requested
* constraints
* assumptions
* unresolved questions

Then draft requirements at the level supported by the information provided.

Separate them into useful groups such as:

* business requirements
* functional requirements
* data requirements
* integration requirements
* security or access requirements
* reporting requirements
* operational requirements
* support requirements
* compliance requirements

Only use categories that are relevant.

Write requirements clearly enough that they can eventually be designed, built, configured, or tested.

For each requirement, include an acceptance condition when the available information supports one.

Do not invent technical design decisions or detailed functionality the business did not request.

Call out anything that needs clarification before the requirements should be treated as complete.

Finish with the most important questions for the business and technical teams to answer together.

## What to provide

Paste the business need, problem statement, current process, desired outcome, stakeholder requests, constraints, known rules, and any other available context.
