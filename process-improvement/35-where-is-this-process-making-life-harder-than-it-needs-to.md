# Where Is This Process Making Life Harder Than It Needs To?

Use this when a process technically works, but people are spending too much time, effort, or frustration getting through it.

## Prompt

Review the current process and identify where it is creating unnecessary work, delay, confusion, or rework.

Look for:

* duplicate entry
* repeated approvals
* unnecessary handoffs
* manual work that could reasonably be automated
* unclear ownership
* waiting time
* bottlenecks
* workarounds
* unnecessary variation
* steps that exist because of an old limitation
* information collected but not used
* errors that create downstream rework
* controls that may no longer match the risk
* places where people leave the process to get the work done

Separate problems with the process itself from problems caused by the system, training, policy, staffing, or another constraint.

For each meaningful problem, explain:

**What is happening**

**Why it creates friction**

**Likely cause**

**Business or user impact**

**Possible improvement**

**What we should understand before changing it**

Prioritize the improvements based on how much they would help compared with how hard they would be to make. Call out anything we can fix fairly easily without creating a new problem somewhere else.

Do not recommend automation simply because a step is manual. First determine whether the step is necessary and whether automating it would actually improve the process.

Finish with:

**Best quick wins**

**Larger improvements worth considering**

**Things we should not change without understanding them better**

## What to provide

Paste the current process, workflow steps, roles, systems, handoffs, pain points, timing, known workarounds, policies, controls, and any available user feedback.
