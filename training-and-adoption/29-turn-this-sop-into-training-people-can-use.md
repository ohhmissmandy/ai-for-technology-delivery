# Turn This SOP Into Training People Can Use

Use this when you already have a procedure or SOP, but it was written as documentation rather than something people can easily learn from.

## Prompt

Review the SOP or procedure below and turn it into practical training.

Start by identifying:

* what the learner needs to be able to do
* what they need to understand before they start
* the main workflow
* important decision points
* common mistakes
* exceptions
* situations where they should stop and ask for help

Then create a training structure that teaches the work in a logical order.

Separate:

**Need to know**

**Need to do**

**Need to practice**

**Useful reference information**

Do not simply shorten the SOP or turn every section into a training slide.

Keep detailed reference information in the SOP or a job aid when learners do not need to memorize it.

Include realistic practice or knowledge checks where they would help confirm that the learner can actually perform the work.

Call out anything in the SOP that is too unclear, incomplete, or contradictory to teach confidently.

## What to provide

Paste the SOP, procedure, work instructions, or other process documentation along with any known information about the intended learners.
