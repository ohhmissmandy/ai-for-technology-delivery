# Create Practice That Feels Like the Real Job

Use this when people need to practice a new process or system before they are expected to use it on their own.

## Prompt

Create realistic practice scenarios from the information below.

Build the practice around the work people will actually need to perform, not around demonstrating every feature of the system.

Include a mix of:

* normal day-to-day situations
* decisions the learner will need to make
* common mistakes
* realistic exceptions
* situations where the learner should stop or escalate
* scenarios where the correct action is not immediately obvious

For each scenario, include:

**Situation**

**What the learner needs to do**

**Information available to them**

**Expected action or decision**

**Why that response is correct**

**Common mistake to watch for**

Do not make every scenario perfectly clean or give the answer away in the setup.

Keep the difficulty appropriate for the learner's actual role.

Finish with a short recommendation for how the practice should be facilitated and how to tell whether the learner is ready to perform the work independently.

## What to provide

Paste the workflow, user role, business rules, common tasks, known exceptions, frequent mistakes, escalation rules, and any existing training material.
