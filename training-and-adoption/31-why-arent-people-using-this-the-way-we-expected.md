# Why Aren't People Using This the Way We Expected?

Use this when a solution has launched but adoption or actual usage does not match what the project expected.

## Prompt

Review the adoption information below and identify the most likely reasons people aren't using it the way we expected.

Look for evidence related to:

* awareness
* understanding
* training
* usability
* process fit
* workload
* access
* system performance
* missing functionality
* workarounds
* competing tools or processes
* manager reinforcement
* incentives
* support
* trust
* resistance to the change

Separate what the evidence actually shows from assumptions about why users are behaving a certain way.

Identify differences between user groups if the adoption pattern is not the same everywhere.

For each likely cause, explain:

**Evidence**

**What we still need to learn**

**Business impact**

**Recommended response**

Do not assume low adoption means users are resistant to change. Look for practical reasons the new approach may be harder, slower, unclear, or less useful than expected.

Finish with the few actions most likely to improve adoption and how we would know whether they worked.

## What to provide

Paste the expected adoption, actual usage, user feedback, support data, training information, workflow changes, known workarounds, and any differences between teams or user groups.
