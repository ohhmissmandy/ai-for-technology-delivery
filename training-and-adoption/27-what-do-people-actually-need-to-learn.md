# What Do People Actually Need to Learn?

Use this when you need to plan training around what people actually need to do, not just what the system can do.

## Prompt

Review the implementation information below and identify what each affected user group actually needs to learn.

For each group, identify:

* what they need to do differently
* what they need to understand
* what they need to practice
* what they only need to be aware of
* what mistakes would create the biggest problems
* what support they may need after training

Separate system knowledge from process knowledge. Someone may know where to click and still not understand when, why, or whether they should take an action.

Then recommend the best way to teach each group. That might be instructor-led training, a demonstration, guided practice, a job aid, a short video, office hours, manager reinforcement, or a mix.

Do not recommend formal training for information that can be handled more effectively through communication or a simple reference tool.

Finish with a practical way to tell whether people can actually do the work, not just whether they showed up for training.

## What to provide

Paste the new process or solution, affected user groups, role differences, workflows, business rules, common tasks, known risks, and any current training plans.
