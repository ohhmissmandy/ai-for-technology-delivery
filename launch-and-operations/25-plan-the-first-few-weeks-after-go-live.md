# Plan the First Few Weeks After Go-Live

Use this when you need a practical hypercare and stabilization plan after launch.

## Prompt

Build a post-launch stabilization plan from the information below.

Identify what should be monitored during the first few days and weeks after go-live, including the areas that matter for this implementation.

Consider:

* system performance
* integrations
* data
* defects
* support volume
* recurring user questions
* workarounds
* business-process problems
* adoption
* vendor issues
* operational handoffs
* unresolved launch items

Define:

* what should be monitored
* who should monitor it
* how often it should be reviewed
* what normal looks like
* what should trigger escalation
* who owns the response

Create a practical meeting and reporting cadence for the stabilization period without creating unnecessary meetings.

Identify the criteria we should use to decide when hypercare can end and normal operational support can take over.

Call out anything that appears likely to remain dependent on the project team after it should have transitioned to operations.

Finish with:

**What we watch closely at first**

**What should trigger escalation**

**What needs to be handed off**

**What tells us the implementation is stable**

## What to provide

Paste the launch plan, known risks, support model, expected volumes, monitoring information, open issues, operational ownership, and any stabilization expectations.
