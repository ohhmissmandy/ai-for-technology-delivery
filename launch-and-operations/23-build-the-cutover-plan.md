# Build the Cutover Plan

Use this when you need to turn a launch into a coordinated sequence of actions, owners, decisions, and checkpoints.

## Prompt

Build a practical cutover plan from the information below.

Organize the work into:

**Before Cutover**

**Cutover Window**

**Validation**

**Business Handoff**

**Post-Launch Monitoring**

For each activity, identify:

* what needs to happen
* owner or responsible team
* dependency
* planned timing or sequence
* evidence that the step is complete
* what happens if the step fails
* whether another activity can continue if it does

Identify the major decision points and any point after which rollback becomes harder, slower, or more disruptive.

Include:

* final readiness checks
* communications
* access or configuration changes
* data activities
* deployment steps
* integration validation
* business validation
* support activation
* monitoring
* escalation paths
* go/no-go checkpoints
* rollback activities where applicable

Only include activities relevant to this launch.

Call out any step that does not have a clear owner, completion condition, dependency, or failure response.

Finish with a short **cutover command view** showing the critical sequence, decision points, and owners.

## What to provide

Paste the launch plan, deployment steps, owners, dependencies, timing, validation requirements, communications, support plan, rollback approach, and other known cutover information.
