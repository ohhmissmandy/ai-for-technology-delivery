# What Happens If Launch Goes Bad?

Use this when you need a practical contingency plan before go-live.

## Prompt

Review the launch plan and identify realistic ways the launch could go wrong or create a business impact we can't accept.

For each one that matters, tell me:

* what could happen
* what we would see
* business or operational impact
* immediate response
* who needs to be involved
* whether we should continue, pause, or roll back
* what information is needed to make that decision

Identify clear trigger points for escalation, pause, or rollback where the information supports them.

Separate problems that can be handled through normal post-launch support from problems serious enough to change the launch decision.

Also identify any contingency that depends on people, access, backups, procedures, or technical capabilities we have not confirmed.

Finish with:

**What we should be prepared for**

**What would make us pause**

**What would make us roll back**

**Who makes those decisions**

## What to provide

Paste the launch plan, known risks, critical business functions, rollback approach, support model, technical dependencies, operational constraints, and known failure scenarios.
