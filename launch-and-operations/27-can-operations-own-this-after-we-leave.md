# Can Operations Own This After We Leave?

Use this when the project is getting close to handoff and you need to know whether the receiving team can actually support the solution without depending on the project team.

## Prompt

Review the operational-readiness information below and assess whether the receiving team is ready to own the solution.

Evaluate:

* day-to-day ownership
* support responsibilities
* procedures and documentation
* access and permissions
* monitoring
* recurring maintenance
* issue triage
* escalation paths
* vendor support
* known limitations
* reporting
* training
* staffing or capacity
* dependencies on project-team members
* ownership of future changes

For each relevant area, identify:

**Ready**

**Needs Work**

**Not Ready**

**Not Enough Information**

Explain what evidence supports the assessment.

Call out anything that still depends on one person knowing how it works instead of having a process the team can actually own.

Identify any knowledge that still exists only in the project team's heads.

Also identify work the project team is still doing that should belong to operations after handoff.

Finish with:

**What operations can own now**

**What still needs to be transferred**

**What could prevent a clean handoff**

**What tells us the project team can step away**

## What to provide

Paste the support model, procedures, ownership information, training status, access, monitoring, escalation paths, vendor arrangements, known issues, recurring work, and current handoff plan.
