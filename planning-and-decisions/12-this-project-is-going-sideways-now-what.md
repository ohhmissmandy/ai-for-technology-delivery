# This Project Is Going Sideways. Now What?

Use this when milestones are slipping, stakeholders are frustrated, problems are multiplying, or the original plan is no longer credible.

## Prompt

Review the current situation and help me determine what is actually happening.

Start by separating **symptoms from likely root causes**. Identify what changed from the original plan, which assumptions no longer hold, and where decisions or dependencies broke down. Also look for problems that are now causing other problems.

Determine:

* what is still recoverable
* what may need to be re-planned
* what work should continue
* what work may need to pause
* which decisions cannot wait
* where leadership or stakeholder intervention is needed
* what commitments may no longer be realistic

If there's more than one realistic way to recover, give me the options and the tradeoffs. Show me what each one means for scope, timing, cost, resources, risk, quality, and the business outcome.

Then create a practical stabilization plan for the next 30 days or other appropriate near-term period.

Finish with:

**What caused this**

**What we need to do now**

**What we need to stop doing**

**What I need leadership to decide**

**What success should look like from here**

Do not preserve the original plan simply because it was the original plan.

## What to provide

Paste the original plan, current status, missed milestones, issues, stakeholder concerns, changes, dependencies, decisions, or other information about what is happening now.
