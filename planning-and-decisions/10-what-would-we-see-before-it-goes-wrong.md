# What Would We See Before It Goes Wrong?

Use this when you already know the major project or adoption risks and want to catch trouble earlier.

## Prompt

Review the risks, goals, KPIs, and project information below.

For each major risk or failure condition, identify the **leading indicators** we might see before the final outcome is affected.

For each indicator, tell me:

* what we would observe
* where the signal might appear
* how early it could reasonably show up
* what threshold or pattern should get our attention
* what action we should take if it appears

Look beyond schedule status. Consider signals from testing, defects, stakeholder behavior, vendor activity, training, user feedback, support requests, resource availability, decision delays, workarounds, adoption, and operational readiness.

Do not create indicators that we cannot realistically observe or measure.

Finish with a short list of the signals that would give us the earliest useful warning.

## What to provide

Paste the major risks, goals, KPIs, project information, adoption measures, or other data that could help identify early warning signs.
