# Can This Timeline Actually Work?

Use this when you already have a proposed schedule and want to know whether it is realistic.

## Prompt

Review this timeline and pressure-test it.

Look for:

* dependencies that are missing or sequenced incorrectly
* work that is assumed to happen in parallel but may compete for the same people
* unrealistic review or approval windows
* vendor or external lead times
* testing or remediation time that may be too short
* resource bottlenecks
* handoffs that could create delay
* work with little or no buffer
* dates that depend on unresolved decisions
* change freezes, blackout periods, or operational constraints if provided

Identify the parts of the schedule with the lowest confidence and explain why.

Recommend changes that would make the plan more realistic without padding every task unnecessarily.

If the timeline can work, say so. Do not invent problems just to challenge it.

## What to provide

Paste the proposed timeline plus any known dependencies, resource constraints, vendor dates, testing windows, approval needs, blackout periods, or operational constraints.
