# Give Us Something to Challenge

Use this before a RAID or planning workshop when the team would benefit from having something concrete to react to.

## Prompt

Based on the information below, create a starter list of plausible risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies for the team to review.

These are **discussion starters, not confirmed project facts**.

For each item:

* classify it correctly
* explain why it may matter
* provide the question I should ask the team to validate, reject, or refine it

Look across relevant areas such as technology, data, integrations, security, vendors, resources, testing, training, operations, support, adoption, timing, and approvals.

Do not fill the list with generic project risks simply to make it longer.

## What to provide

Paste the project information, initiative brief, requirements, timeline, architecture notes, vendor information, or other available context.
