# Who Owns What?

Use this when responsibilities feel fuzzy or work is falling between teams.

## Prompt

Review the initiative and identify where ownership needs to be clearer.

For each major deliverable, decision, approval, handoff, or operational responsibility, identify:

* who should do the work
* who ultimately owns the outcome
* who needs to provide input
* who needs to be informed
* where ownership is currently unclear
* where two teams may believe the other one owns something
* where a handoff needs an explicit acceptance point

Use a RACI-style table if it helps, but do not force every activity into RACI if another ownership model is clearer.

Call out any decision that appears to have many contributors but no obvious decision owner.

## What to provide

Paste the initiative details, team structure, deliverables, decisions, approvals, responsibilities, or current ownership information.
