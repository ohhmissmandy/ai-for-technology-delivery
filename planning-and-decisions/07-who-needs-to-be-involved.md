# Who Needs to Be Involved?

Use this when you need to understand the stakeholder landscape around an initiative.

## Prompt

Based on the project information below, build a practical stakeholder map.

For each relevant person or group, identify:

* their role in the initiative
* how they're affected
* what they care about or need from the project
* what the project needs from them
* decisions they own or influence
* likely concerns
* how and when they need to be involved

Then identify:

**People who need alignment before work moves forward**

**People who could block or delay delivery**

**People who could accelerate adoption**

**People who may be easy to overlook but will feel the impact day to day**

Do not assume every senior leader needs constant communication or every end user belongs in every meeting.

## What to provide

Paste the project information, known teams, business areas, decision-makers, users, vendors, or other stakeholder context.
