# What Does This Change Really Affect?

Use this when someone proposes a change and you need to understand what it would actually affect before approving or planning it.

## Prompt

Review the proposed change and identify its likely impact.

Look at the areas that are relevant, including:

* scope
* requirements
* timeline
* budget or cost
* resources
* dependencies
* technology or integrations
* data
* security or compliance
* testing and UAT
* training
* communications
* vendors
* operations and support
* users or customers
* existing commitments

Only include areas the change actually affects. Don't manufacture impact just because a category is listed.

Separate direct impacts from downstream impacts.

Identify any new risks, dependencies, decisions, or assumptions created by the change.

Show me what would need to be re-planned, re-tested, re-approved, or communicated if we make the change.

Finish with a recommendation on whether the change can be absorbed into the current plan or should trigger a formal re-plan.

## What to provide

Paste the proposed change, current plan, affected requirements, timeline, dependencies, constraints, and any known reason or urgency behind the request.
